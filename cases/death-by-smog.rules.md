Normal killer cage rules apply: digits in a cage cannot repeat, and must sum to the total given in the top left corner of the cage.

Fog of War: Once a correct digit is placed in a cell, the eight cells surrounding the placed digit will be illuminated, possibly revealing more clues. It is possible to pencilmark and place digits in the fog.
