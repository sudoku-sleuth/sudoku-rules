RIVER - Split points of the loop cannot occur on the blue river (loop segments must form a "bridge" when crossing the river).

CASTLES - A digit in a castle gives the number of loop segments in the same 3x3 box as that castle. A loop segment is counted if at least one of its cells appears within the 3x3 box. Castles may be on or off the loop.

FLAGS - Each flag touches one loop cell and one non-loop cell. Digits joined by a white flag must be consecutive, and digits joined by a black flag must be in a 2:1 ratio.
