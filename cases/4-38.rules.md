Normal Sudoku rules apply.
Digits in killer cages must sum to the value in the top left hand corner of the cage. Digits cannot repeat within cages.
Green lines are German Whispers lines. Adjacent digits along the line must differ in value by at least 5.
Digits along a between line must be strictly between the values of the digits in the circles at the end of the line.
Digits along an arrow must sum to the value of the digit in the arrow's circle.
Cells joined by white dots must be consecutive in value. Cells joined by black dots must be in a ratio of 2:1. Not all dots are given.
Cells connected by Xs sum to 10. Not all Xs are given.
