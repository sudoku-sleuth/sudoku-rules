Today Nala has been tasked with uploading our puzzle to LMD. She has no clue where to begin. Can you help her out a bit?

Normal 9x9 sudoku rules apply. Place the digits 1-9 in every row, column and 3x3 box exactly once each.

Guide Nala’s paw to the activate button. The path from the paw to the activate button acts as a German whisper line. Digits along the line differ by at least 5. The path is orthogonally connected and not allowed to touch itself orthogonally or branch.

Along the path help Nala to fulfill the following requirements in order to successfully post our puzzle:

- The title should appear in bold letters.
- The link to the puzzle needs to be clickable.
- The image needs to appear below the linked puzzle. (It cannot be uploaded if it’s too big!)
- Each section needs to be separated by a paragraph to make it look prettier.

Digits separated by a white dot are consecutive and digits separated by a black dot are in a 1:2 ratio. Not all possible dots are given. (There is no negative constraint.)

Don’t fail this mission or else we won’t have a puzzle to show for the first of April!
