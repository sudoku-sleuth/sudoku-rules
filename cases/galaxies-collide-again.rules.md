Normal sudoku rules apply.

Divide the grid into two galaxies of orthogonally connected cells. No 2x2 area may be completely within one galaxy. Both galaxies must be rotationally symmetrical through 180°.

A digit in a green circle indicates how many cells in its sudoku box are in its galaxy.

A digit in a red square indicates how many cells in its sudoku box are NOT in its galaxy.

One of the two galaxies is special. In that galaxy, for any two orthogonally adjacent cells, dividing one digit by the other always gives an exact integer (one is a multiple of the other.)
