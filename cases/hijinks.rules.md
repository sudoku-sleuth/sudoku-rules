Normal sudoku rules apply.

Along each line, the hexagon is referred to as 'position 1', the next cell along is 'position 2' and so on. Digits never repeat along a line.

PURPLE LINES are thermos. Digits along thermos always increase as you move away from the hexagon. 
Eg: 24579 would be a valid thermo.

TURQUOISE LINES are index lines. On an index line, the digit in the Nth position indicates the position along the line where the digit N appears.
Eg: 32154 would be a valid index line. The 3 in the 1st position indicates there is a 1 in the 3rd position, and so on.

LIGHT BROWN LINES are hitlines. Digits can be in any order on a hitline, but the digit in the hexagon  indicates how many digits along the hitline match their position number. 
Eg: A hitline might read 27315. The 2 on the hexagon indicates that exactly two digits match their position number; in this case, 3 in the 3rd position and 5 in the 5th position.
