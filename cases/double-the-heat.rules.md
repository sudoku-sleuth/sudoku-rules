**Sudoku, Thermometers, Ratio Pairs**

- Standard sudoku rules apply: The digits 1 through 9 appear in every row, column, and box.
- Thermometers: Digits on Thermometers are strictly increasing from bulb (grey circle) to tip (along the grey line).
- Ratio pairs: Digits in cells separated by a black dot are in a 2:1 ratio. Not all dots are necessarily given.
