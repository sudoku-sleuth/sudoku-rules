In this puzzle, coloured lines are 'edge thermos'... these run along cell edges.

Any cell edge covered by a thermo contains a thermo spot. The value of a thermo spot equals the sum of the digits in the two cells it sits between. Values of spots along a thermo strictly increase moving away from the bulb (the large spot at one end.) Each thermo is a different colour, and they don't overlap or intersect each other.
