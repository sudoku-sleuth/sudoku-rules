**Sudoku, Whisper-5, Whisper-4, Kropki Pairs**

- Standard sudoku rules apply: The digits 1 through 9 appear in every row, column, and box.
- Whisper-5: Adjacent digits along a Whisper-5 line must differ by at least five. In this puzzle, the line forming the numbers are Whisper-5 lines.
- Whisper-4: Adjacent digits along a Whisper-4 line must differ by at least four. In this puzzle, the line forming the arrow is a Whisper-4 line.
- Kropki Pairs: Digits in cells separated by a black dot are in a 1:2 ratio. Digits in cells separated by a white dot are consecutive.
