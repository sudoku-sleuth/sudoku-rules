LINE RULES:

• EQUAL SEGMENT LINE: Along a BLUE LINE, each segment has the same total.

• SEGMENT NABNER: Along a yellow line, any two segment totals anywhere on the line may not be consecutive or the same (eg: if there is a segment whose cells sum to 12, then no other segment may have a total of 11, 12 or 13.)
