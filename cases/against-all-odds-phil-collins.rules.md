'And you comin' back to me is against all odds
It's the chance I've got to take...' Phil Collins (1984). Normal Sudoku rules apply. ROTATIONAL SYMMETRY- Numbers 180 degrees apart in the puzzle sum to 10. ODD LINES- Numbers on ORANGE lines are all odd. Also, the average of all numbers on each orange line is an odd number.  EVEN LINES- Numbers on BLUE lines are all even. Also, the average of all numbers on each blue line is an even number. The averages of ALL lines are different than one another. KILLER CAGES- Numbers in cages sum to 35.
