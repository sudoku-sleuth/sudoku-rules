Happy Birthday, Missing!

Standard sudoku constraints apply: Every row, column, and box must contain a complete set of the digits 1-9.

German whispers: Adjacent digits on green lines must differ by at least 5.

Renban lines: Digits on purple lines for a nonrepeating set of consecutive digits in any order.

Kropki dots: Digits separated by a white dot are consecutive. Not all possible white dots are given.
