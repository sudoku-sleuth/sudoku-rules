Every question mark represents a different constraint.

Nabner: Digits on the golden line cannot repeat, and no two digits on the line can be consecutive, regardless of their order along the line.
German whisper line: Adjacent digits along the green line must differ by at least 5.
Parity line: Adjacent digits along the red line must alternate in parity (odd/even).
Thermometer: Digits along the grey line must increase from the bulb to the tip, which can be on either end and are to be deduced.
Region sum line: Box borders divide the blue line into segments with the same sum.
Combo line: The turquoise line acts as both a German whisper line and a Region sum line, as described above.

Digits separated by a black dot must be in a 1:2 ratio. All black dots are given.
