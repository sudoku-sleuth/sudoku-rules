Normal Sudoku rules apply. SKYSCRAPER SUMS- Numbers inside the grid represent a building of that height, at that location. Smaller buildings cannot be seen if they are behind any larger ones. Numbers outside the grid are the sum of the HEIGHTS of all buildings that can be seen when looking horizontally or vertically into the grid.  KROPKI PAIRS- Numbers with a white dot between them are consecutive. Not all possible dots are shown. Do not use the following HINT if you want a more difficult solve: Except for the nines, which are NOT shaded, numbers shaded light green can be seen from the left or right skyscraper clue points. Except for the nine, numbers shaded dark green can be seen from the top or bottom clue points.
